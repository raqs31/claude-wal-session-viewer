## Goal

A `tok` record becomes a hidden index entry that sets `out` / `res` on its target row, and `Row` carries the numbers (plan.md Phase 6 §3). Add a server-level case to `scripts/query.test.ts` that a call row and a Stop row return `out` / `res` in `/events`.

## Non-goals

- No token filter, `sort=tokens` or `tokens` stream frames (Phase 7).
- No change to the observer.

## Context

Plan: `.ai/changes/wal-observer-pipeline/plan.md`, Phase 6 §3 and "One seq space for patches".

Build on T15 (`tok` records in the target) and T8 (`tail()` in `scripts/wal-index.ts` reads the target).

Contract (copied from plan.md Phase 6 §3):
- A `tok` record becomes a hidden entry. It sets `out` / `res` on its target entry, found through a `tuid` → call seq map and a `woff` → Stop seq map. `woff` is the WAL offset; `IndexEntry.off` is the target offset.
- `IndexEntry` (`scripts/wal-index.ts:42`) gains `out: number | null`, `res: number | null` and `hidden: boolean`. Every entry sets all three, to keep one hidden class.
- `Row` (`shared/api.ts:30`) gains `out?: number` and `res?: number`, set only when present.
- Hidden entries count in no `total`, `counts`, facets or rows.
- `tok` entries take a seq like rows, so the stream's `id:` vector covers them. Do not add a second position vector.

Do not commit; the orchestrator commits each phase after its review.
