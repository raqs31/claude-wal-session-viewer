## Context

Intent (plan.md Phase 5 §2, copied): "A modal on the native `<dialog>` (`showModal`, Escape cancels) listing the count and session names (scrollable), the busy ones marked as skipped (`live` / `not ended`), with Cancel and a confirm button; an optional typed-`yes` field that keeps the confirm button disabled until it matches."

Its props are the contract T17 (Delete picked / Delete N filtered) and T18 (Delete forever, Empty trash) build on: a title, the confirm button label, the listed items (name plus an optional skipped label), an optional typed-`yes` requirement, `onConfirm`, `onCancel`. Record the final props signature in the Result.
