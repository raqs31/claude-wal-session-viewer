## Goal

The Events head "N recorded" counts only visible rows (call, turn and other rows), never hidden `tok` entries. The no-WAL session 6a4be781 shows "showing 37 · 37 recorded", not "70 recorded".

## Non-goals

- Do not change `head`, the `vector()` format, frame `id:` lines or `Last-Event-ID` handling. `head` stays the stream cursor over all entries.
- Do not change StatsHeader.tsx or EventsTab.tsx: the `Stats.head` field keeps its name and now holds the visible count.
- Do not touch scripts/trash.ts.
