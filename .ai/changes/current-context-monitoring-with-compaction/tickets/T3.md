## Goal

Measure the hook's context-step overhead on the largest real transcript under `~/.claude/projects`, record the numbers in a new `## Review` section at the end of plan.md, and add a guard in the hook only if the median overhead exceeds 5 ms.

## Non-goals

- No committed benchmark code: scripts and copies live in a temp dir (`mktemp -d`) outside the repo and are deleted after.
- No guard when the median overhead is 5 ms or less.
- Do not tick plan.md Progress boxes; do not commit.

## Context

Board decision (2026-10-22, overrides plan.md's "generated transcript"): the benchmark uses the largest real transcript under `~/.claude/projects`, read-only. At planning time that is `/home/mraczynski/.claude/projects/-home-mraczynski-IdeaProjects/ba91cbd2-8f37-405f-b945-939970a9e544.jsonl` (42.4 MB; re-find it with `find ~/.claude/projects -name '*.jsonl' ! -name '*.wal.jsonl' -printf '%s %p\n' | sort -n | tail -1`). It is below the plan's 50 MB; record the real size.

Read-only, and why a copy is mandatory: `walPath` (`plugins/wal-hook/hooks/wal-hook.mjs:63`) writes the WAL to `<transcript_path minus .jsonl>.wal.jsonl`, i.e. beside the transcript, where a real WAL may already exist. So `cp` the transcript into a `mktemp -d` dir and point `transcript_path` in every benchmark payload at the copy; never write, move or append to anything under `~/.claude/projects`. Do not print transcript contents; only sizes and timings.

Method (plan.md Phase 2): run the hook 30x each with a PostToolUse payload (reads the tail) and a PreToolUse payload (no read) against the same copy; overhead = difference of medians. Worst case: a second copy with a 4 MB user line appended (no assistant line in the last 4 MB), same 30x pair. Record in `## Review`: file size, median and p95 for each of the four runs, and the overhead for normal and worst case.

Guard (only if median overhead > 5 ms): in `plugins/wal-hook/hooks/wal-hook.mjs`, cut the parse cost cheaply, e.g. skip `JSON.parse` for lines that do not contain `"assistant"`, or lower the cap; choose by what the measurement shows, re-measure and record both. T1's tests must still pass unchanged.
